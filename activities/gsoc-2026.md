## Contributor Guidance

We welcome contributors of all skill levels!  Here's how to get started:

1. **Get Familiar with eweOS:** The best way to contribute is to understand our project.  Try installing eweOS (you can use a virtual machine).
2. **Build Your Development Environment:** Try building a package under eweOS!
3. **Join the Community:**  Introduce yourself in our Matrix/Telegram group or maillist.  Ask questions, share your ideas, and get to know other members of the community.
4. **Explore the Codebase:**  Our source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/eweOS).  Browse the code, understand the structure, and identify areas where you might be able to contribute.
5. **Understand the proposal process**: Read Google's Contributor Guide and follow the instructions.
6.  **Proposal Guidelines:**
    *   **Clear and Concise:**  Your proposal should be easy to understand and clearly outline your project goals, deliverables, and timeline.
    *   **Realistic Scope:**  Choose a project that is achievable within the GSoC timeframe.  Break down large tasks into smaller, manageable milestones.
    *   **Demonstrate Understanding:** Show that you understand the relevant parts of eweOS and how your project will integrate with the system.
    *   **Personal Background:** Briefly describe your relevant skills and experience.
    *   **Timeline:** Provide a realistic week by week timeline for your project, including specific tasks and milestones.
